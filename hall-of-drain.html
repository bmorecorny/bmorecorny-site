What earns a place?

A Hall of Drain record requires an IN HOLE result at the official 100-foot distance, recorded in a completed official 100-Footer game and approved through the official verification process. The record must include the required evidence and confirmations.

ON BOARD is a separate achievement. Alternate-distance games, pending reviews, rejected attempts, unverified throws, and test results do not qualify. No shortcuts. No honorary entries.
